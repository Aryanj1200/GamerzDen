💡 Moving data between local file & online?
1. Open the version that has your data → Settings → click Export All Data → save the .json file.
2. Open the version that needs it → Settings → click Import Data → pick that .json file.
Everything (menu, partners, sessions, history, reports, settings) transfers instantly.

💡 How partner pricing works:
For each item from this partner, you set two prices:
• Customer Price — what customer pays (you control this)
• Partner Cost — what you actually owe the partner per unit
Your margin = Customer Price − Partner Cost. The Partner Payments page tracks how much you owe based on Partner Cost × quantity.
